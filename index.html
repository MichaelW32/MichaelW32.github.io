MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

Michael (Zihan) Wang

Mechanical engineering student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, graduating in May 2027.

My interests center on mechanical design, mechanics, and computational engineering. I enjoy working through the full design process, from CAD models and analysis to building prototypes, testing them, and revising the design.

My experience includes non-contact laser measurement equipment at Proton Products, bio-inspired structures research at UC San Diego, and student vehicle teams at Illinois. My current projects explore walking mechanisms and machine learning for aerodynamic prediction.
